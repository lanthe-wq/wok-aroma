## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro, static output, with the motion layer (GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis) as plain client modules. The owner picked "Astro or Next.js" and left the choice to the builder. Astro ships no framework runtime, builds to flat files any static host serves, and keeps a one-page restaurant site cheap to load on a phone. Deploy target: undecided.

## Users

People in and around Indirapuram, Ghaziabad (Delhi NCR) deciding what to eat now: students, office-goers, families, and night-owls. Inferred from the address, the 24-hour listing and the delivery radius; not confirmed with the owner. Most arrive from the Google Maps listing, Swiggy or Zomato, on a phone. Their job: "is this the place, what does it cost, how do I get it?" in a few seconds, then call, order, or walk over.

## Product Purpose

Wok Aroma is an Indo-Chinese street-style restaurant and delivery kitchen: momos, noodles, fried rice, manchurian, chilli starters, soups, Maggi, sandwiches, shakes. The owner's own printed tagline is "Authentic Crazy Chinese Food", and the logo line is "Savor the Flavors". The site turns a Maps listing into a place people can feel before they arrive, and moves them to one of four actions: directions, call to order, order online (Swiggy / Zomato), read the menu.

## Positioning

Cooked in a wok, open every hour: the Google Maps listing says "Open 24 hours". The name claims the smell first (wok + aroma); the menu claims the price (many items between ₹119 and ₹349, combos from ₹119). A neighbouring restaurant can say "fresh"; a place that is lit and cooking at 3 a.m. can prove it.

## Operating Context

Evenings and late nights, hunger, a phone in one hand. Orders arrive by walk-in, by phone call, or through Swiggy and Zomato; the kitchen prints or writes an order slip for each. The owner will edit facts (hours, prices, dishes, links) without touching layout.

That printed logo file was not supplied. ## Product Principles

1. Name the unknowns, never invent them. A link hidden until supplied beats a plausible fake.
2. The smell sells. Lead with heat, flame and aroma as sensation, then with the price. Make no claims about awards, ratings, history or sourcing.
3. Phone first. Call, directions, order and the menu stay within thumb reach on every screen.
4. Show the real offer: 24 hours, the printed prices, free delivery within 3 km above ₹500.
5. Motion is the signature, never the gate. The page reads without JavaScript and calms down for reduced-motion users.
6. One source of truth. Editing a fact means editing one file.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, full `prefers-reduced-motion` path, no autoplay audio, meaningful alt text and landmarks. Veg and non-veg are marked by shape and label as well as colour.
